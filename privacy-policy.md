# Privacy Policy

**Little Gods** (working title)
**Effective date:** 26 November 2026
**Last updated:** 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Little Gods mobile application for Android (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by **Rubesh R** ("we", "us", "our").

**In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you set up in the App stays on your phone.**

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 We collect no personal data
The App does not ask for, collect, or send us:

- your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details;
- your location;
- your contacts, photos, files, messages or call logs;
- anything shown on your screen;
- usage analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

The App has no user accounts and no sign-in, and we run no servers that receive information from it.

### 1.2 What stays on your device
To work, the App saves a small amount of information **only on your phone**, in the App's private storage:

- your chosen language;
- the little gods you added and where you placed them on the screen;
- your favourites, and which gods have their divine companion with them;
- your settings (size, speed, sounds, speech bubbles, morning-blessing time, festival options and similar);
- whether onboarding is complete, and simple counters, such as how many speech bubbles were shown today, or the day a short video opened an extra slot or the companions.

This information never leaves your device. Only you can see it, and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it permanently.

### 1.3 No internet access
The current version of the App does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send information over the internet. If a future version adds the optional services in Section 4, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

---

## 2. Permissions the App uses, and why

| Permission | Why it is needed | What it does **not** do |
|---|---|---|
| **Display over other apps** (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`) | To draw your little gods on top of other apps, which is the App's main feature. | It cannot read, record, capture or interact with the content of other apps or your screen. |
| **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`), optional | To show the ongoing "little gods on screen" notification, the morning blessing and festival wishes. | No marketing or third-party notifications. You can refuse or turn this off at any time. |
| **Foreground service** (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE`) | To keep the little gods floating while you use other apps, with a visible notification so you can rest or turn them off. | It does not monitor your activity. |
| **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | Only if you turn on "Start when the phone turns on", to bring the little gods back after a restart. | Nothing else. |
| **Vibration** (`VIBRATE`) | A light tap of haptic feedback when you pick up a little god. | Nothing else. |

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's Settings > Apps > Little Gods.

---

## 3. Speaking blessings aloud (optional)
If you turn on "Speak blessings aloud", the App passes the blessing text to your phone's **built-in text-to-speech engine** (for example, Google Speech Services). The text is spoken on your device. The App does not send it to us. Your text-to-speech provider's own privacy terms apply to that engine.

---

## 4. Third-party services (planned, optional)
The following Google services may be added in a future version. Where they are used, the data they handle is processed by **Google**, under Google's privacy policies, and not by us.

### 4.1 Google Play Billing (purchases)
If you buy Premium or the "Remove ads" option, the purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play**. We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details. The App only receives confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid, so it can unlock the features you paid for. See the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

### 4.2 Google AdMob (advertising, free version only)
The free version may show ads provided by **Google AdMob**. Premium users and buyers of "Remove ads" see no ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location, as described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Where the law requires it (for example, for users in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

If these services are added, this section and the App's Google Play "Data safety" form will be updated to list exactly what is collected.

## 5. Sharing of information
We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because we do not collect it. When you use "Share app", Android's own share menu opens and you choose where to share a link to the App. We see nothing of this.

---

## 6. Children's privacy
The App is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If ads are added, they will be configured in line with Google Play's Families and advertising policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help.

---

## 7. Data security
Because your settings are stored only on your device, in storage that other apps cannot read, their security is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock, encryption and Android's app sandbox). We recommend keeping your phone's software up to date.

---

## 8. Data retention and deletion
Your data stays on your device until you:

- clear the App's data (Settings > Apps > Little Gods > Storage > Clear data), or
- uninstall the App.

Either action deletes it permanently. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

---

## 9. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**, the EU/UK **GDPR**, or US state laws such as the **CCPA/CPRA**. These include the right to access, correct or delete it, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect or hold personal data, there is normally nothing to access or delete. You remain in full control of what is stored on your device, as described in Section 8. For any request or question, contact us using the details below.
